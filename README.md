### **1. Project Summary & Problem Statement**

[cite_start]The core challenge AapdaSetu addresses is the **significant information-to-action gap** in disaster management[cite: 4]. [cite_start]Currently, data exists in distributed fragments (weather forecasts, ground reports, historical records) [cite: 5][cite_start], but it's not synthesized into timely, actionable intelligence for authorities and citizens[cite: 4].

* [cite_start]**Pre-Disaster Gap**: There is a lack of precise, hyperlocal, and context-aware early warnings[cite: 6].
* [cite_start]**Post-Disaster Gap**: Authorities struggle with a real-time, consolidated view of the ground reality and do not know how to prioritize resource allocation efficiently[cite: 7, 8].

### **5. Citizen Usage Guide**

[cite_start]AapdaSetu is designed to provide immediate safety alerts and actionable instructions to citizens with minimal steps[cite: 80].

**Receiving an Alert (Pre-Disaster/In-Crisis)**

1.  [cite_start]**Personalized Alert**: As a logged-in resident, you will instantly receive a **personalized safety alert** based on your location[cite: 27]. This occurs in real-time as the Admin approves the alert through the HITL process.
2.  [cite_start]**Actionable Steps**: The alert card provides a brief description [cite: 14] [cite_start]and a checklist of **AI-enhanced safety recommendations** (e.g., "Charge Phones," "Secure Gas Valves")[cite: 14].
3.  [cite_start]**Track Status**: You can track the status and updates of the active alert[cite: 28].

**Requesting Help (During/Post-Disaster)**

1.  [cite_start]**Access Help**: Click the 'Report Status and Request Help' button on the portal[cite: 29].
2.  [cite_start]**Auto-Fetch Location**: The system automatically uses your device's **GPS API** to fetch your current location[cite: 14, 36].
3.  [cite_start]**Categorize Emergency**: Select the category of your emergency (e.g., Flood, Medical, Infrastructure)[cite: 14].
4.  [cite_start]**AI Chatbot Guidance**: Get guidance from the **AI-powered Chatbot**[cite: 30]. [cite_start]The Chatbot processes your query and the current disaster context to provide a recommendation of the appropriate help response[cite: 14].
5.  **Submission**: Your report is submitted to the Admin portal, where it is instantly visualized on the Live Map for response coordination.
